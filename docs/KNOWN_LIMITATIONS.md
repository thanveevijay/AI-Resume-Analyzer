# Known Limitations

- Keyword matching cannot understand all synonyms or context.
- A detected skill mention does not establish proficiency.
- The heuristic score is not calibrated against hiring outcomes.
- PDF extraction quality varies with layout and scanned documents.
- Section heading detection is intentionally simple.
- Contact masking is best-effort and is not anonymization.
- The tool does not assess communication, teamwork, or project impact.

Use the report as a checklist for human review, not as a candidate ranking.
