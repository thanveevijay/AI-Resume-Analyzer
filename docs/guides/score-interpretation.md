# Interpreting Resume Scores

A resume score is a heuristic comparison against one supplied job description. It is not a probability of receiving an interview or an estimate of candidate quality.

## Recommended interpretation
1. Review evidence for each matched skill.
2. Check whether missing terms are genuinely required.
3. Inspect experience and project context.
4. Correct parsing mistakes before editing the resume.
5. Re-run the analysis after meaningful revisions.

Never optimize a resume by adding skills or experience the candidate does not have.
